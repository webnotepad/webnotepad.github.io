    Our story

    A notepad built on one principle.

    Notepad Online was created with a single obsession: what if a notepad just worked? No popups asking you to sign up. No complex menus to navigate before you can write a single word.

    We believe writing tools should be invisible. The best notepad is the one you forget is there — it just captures your thoughts the moment they arrive. That's what WebNotePad does.

    Your notes live in your browser, not our servers. We don't collect your data. We don't read your notes. We don't monetize your attention. WebNotePad is, and always will be, 100% free and private.
