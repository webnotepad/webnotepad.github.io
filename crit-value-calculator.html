Understanding CV

What Is Crit Value?

Crit Value, or CV, is a quick way to judge how good an artifact's critical stats are. The formula is simple: Crit Rate × 2 + Crit DMG. Crit Rate is doubled because one point of Crit Rate is worth about two points of Crit DMG in a healthy 1:2 build.

With CV you can compare artifacts that have different mixes, such as one with high Crit Rate and low Crit DMG against another with the reverse. Whichever has the higher CV usually adds more damage, as long as your Crit Rate is not already at 100%.

The roll analyzer goes one step further and estimates how many upgrade rolls went into each crit stat and how strong those rolls were.

Artifact Guide

Use CV Wisely.

CV is a guide, not a rule. Use it together with your character's needs.

Tips

  • Stop counting Crit Rate once you reach about 100%. Further rate is wasted, so choose the capped-rate option to see Crit DMG only.
  • A circlet with a crit main stat already contributes a lot, so judge it by its substats plus the main stat shown separately.
  • Two artifacts with the same CV can still differ in other useful stats such as ATK%, ER or Elemental Mastery.
  • Aim for a Crit Rate to Crit DMG ratio near 1:2 on your final build.
