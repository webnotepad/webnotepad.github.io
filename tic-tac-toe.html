What is it?

The Free Online Tic-Tac-Toe Game

Tic-Tac-Toe (also known as Noughts and Crosses) is one of the oldest and most universally loved games in the world. Played on a simple 3×3 grid, it takes seconds to learn but can be played for hours. Two players alternate placing X's and O's, racing to line up three of their marks in a row.

Our free online Tic-Tac-Toe brings this timeless classic into the browser with a beautiful, modern interface. Play with a friend in 2-player pass-and-play mode — perfect for a quick match on a shared phone or tablet — or challenge our computer AI across three difficulty levels.

The Easy AI plays randomly, making it great for young children or first-timers. The Medium AI will block your obvious winning moves and take its own opportunities, offering a fair challenge. The Hard AI uses a perfect minimax algorithm that evaluates every possible move — it is mathematically unbeatable. The best you can achieve against Hard is a draw.

Whether you're a kid learning the game, a parent looking for a quick family activity, or an adult wanting a mindful 30-second break, this browser-based Tic-Tac-Toe delivers a clean, ad-free, and thoroughly enjoyable experience. Track your wins across the session with the built-in scoreboard, and try to beat the unbeatable AI.
