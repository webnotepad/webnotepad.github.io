The general rule is that one litre of paint covers about 10–12 square metres (or one gallon covers about 350–400 square feet) per coat. Measure your walls, subtract doors and windows, multiply by the number of coats, then divide by the coverage rate.

Most interior walls need two coats for full coverage and durability. If you're painting over a dark colour with a light colour, or using a dramatically different colour, you may need three coats. A primer coat may also be needed.
