Frequently Asked Questions.

Fuel cost is calculated by dividing the trip distance by your vehicle's fuel efficiency to find the fuel needed, then multiplying by the fuel price. For example, a 300-mile trip at 30 MPG needs 10 gallons; at $3.50/gallon, that's $35.

MPG (miles per gallon) measures how far you can drive on one gallon — higher is better. L/100km (litres per 100 kilometres) measures how much fuel you use over 100 km — lower is better. Our calculator converts between them automatically.
