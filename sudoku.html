What is it?

The Free Online Sudoku

Sudoku is one of the most popular logic puzzles in the world. Played on a 9×9 grid split into nine 3×3 boxes, the goal is simple: fill every empty cell with a digit from 1 to 9 so that each row, each column and each 3×3 box contains every digit exactly once. Given cells — called givens — are already filled in and can never be changed.

What makes Sudoku special is that no arithmetic or guessing is needed. Every well-formed puzzle has exactly one solution that can be reached through pure logic. Our free Sudoku uses a solver that verifies uniqueness before presenting a puzzle, so you never have to worry about a board that has multiple valid answers.

The game ships with four difficulty levels. Easy leaves around 40 givens and can be solved with basic scanning techniques. Medium drops to about 32 givens and adds more hidden singles. Hard leaves about 28 givens and requires techniques like pointing pairs and box-line reduction. Expert leaves only 24 givens and demands advanced methods like X-Wings and swordfish.

To help you at every stage, the game includes a full toolset: pencil notes for tracking candidates, an auto-notes button that fills every legal candidate in one press, undo for the last 200 moves, and a hint system that can reveal a single cell or a whole digit. Mistake detection highlights errors the moment they happen, so you can learn from them without guessing.
