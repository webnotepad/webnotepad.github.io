Questions

Click speed test FAQ.

An average user clicks about 3–5 CPS with a mouse. Competitive gamers often reach 6–8 CPS, and skilled players using jitter or butterfly clicking can exceed 10 CPS. Any score above 5 CPS is considered strong for a casual user.

The fastest documented click rates exceed 14 clicks per second using specialised techniques such as drag clicking or butterfly clicking. Records vary between clicker communities, and results depend heavily on the mouse, its polling rate and the browser.

CPS is your average clicks per second across the challenge. Peak CPS is the highest number of clicks you landed in any single second. Consistency measures how evenly you clicked — a higher value means steadier performance rather than short bursts.

All clicking, scoring and PDF generation happens locally in your browser. Your results are stored only in your own browser's local storage so you can track your best score between sessions. Nothing is ever uploaded to a server.

Relax your hand and use shorter finger travel. Practise regularly, keep your wrist steady, use a mouse with a high polling rate (1000 Hz or more), and try techniques like butterfly or jitter clicking only when they feel comfortable. Warm up with a 5-second test before attempting a record.

Click speed is affected by many factors: hand fatigue, mouse hardware, browser performance, operating system scheduling and even how warm your hands are. Scores naturally fluctuate a little between attempts — that's normal.
