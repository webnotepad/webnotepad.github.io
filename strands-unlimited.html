What is it?

The Free Strands Unlimited Game

Strands Unlimited is a free online word search game inspired by the NYT Strands puzzle. You are given a grid of letters and must find hidden theme words by connecting adjacent letters. But there's a twist: one special word, the spangram, spans the entire grid and reveals the theme.

What sets Strands Unlimited apart is that there is no daily limit. The original game releases one puzzle per day; here, every press of the New Puzzle button instantly loads a fresh grid. You can binge ten puzzles in a row, take your time on a single hard one, or use the game as a warm-up before the daily.

The game keeps track of your win streak, games played and games won, all stored locally in your browser. Every puzzle gives you three hints, so there is a real risk/reward decision every time you need a little help.

When you finish, you can share a Wordle-style emoji grid showing your progress and any hints used, without spoiling the actual words. It is the same satisfying share you know from the daily game, but available any time you want.
