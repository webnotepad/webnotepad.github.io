Positive thinking is often sold as a mood. In practice it works better as a practice: noticing what is already going well, choosing a response instead of a reaction, and giving other people the benefit of the doubt. These quotes are chosen for that quieter work.

How to Use These Quotes

Positive life quotes work best as a daily one-minute habit. Pick one, paste it into an online diary, and write a single sentence about where you saw it in your day. After two weeks, read back — the entries become a record of what you actually notice.

Attention is a limited resource. What you choose to notice, repeatedly, becomes the shape of your life.— WebNotePad Team
