What is it?

The Free Online Wordle Game

Wordle is a daily word puzzle that challenges you to guess a five-letter word in six attempts. After each guess, the tiles change color to give you clues: green for correct letters in the right position, yellow for correct letters in the wrong position, and gray for letters not in the word.

Our free Wordle game includes hundreds of common five-letter English words, so you get a fresh challenge every time you play. The target word is randomly selected, which means you can play unlimited games and practice your vocabulary and deduction skills.

Whether you're a word game enthusiast, a student looking to improve your English, or just someone who enjoys a quick brain teaser, this online Wordle is perfect for you. It's mobile-friendly, so you can play on your phone, tablet, or desktop. The on-screen keyboard makes it easy to tap in your guesses on touch devices.

No signup, no downloads, no ads blocking your view — just a clean, beautiful word puzzle game that works right in your browser. Play as many times as you like and see if you can beat your streak!
