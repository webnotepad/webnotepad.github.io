How to Use Hints Without Spoiling the Fun

Hints are most useful when they match how stuck you are. A good rule is to use the smallest help that gets you moving, then stop. The steps below work for every game on this page.

  1. Make one more honest attempt first. Take a real guess before you open anything. You often find the solution when you restate the problem.
  2. Open the lightest hint only. Every page orders hints from a vague nudge to a near-giveaway, so start at the top.
  3. Read the explanation after you solve. The strategy sections describe why the answer was hard, which helps you spot the same trick next time.
  4. Keep a short note. Write down the pattern you missed. A running list is the fastest route to improvement.
The goal is not a perfect streak. It is a few minutes of good thinking each day. Use the hints that keep the game fun and ignore the rest.— Daily Puzzle Desk

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Hints & Answers page?

It is a daily hub for the most popular word and logic puzzles. Each puzzle has its own page with layered hints first and the full answer behind a tap, so you can choose how much help you want.

Which puzzles are covered?

Wordle, NYT Connections, NYT Strands, NYT Spelling Bee and Contexto. Each page includes the puzzle number, the date and the full solution for today’s game.

Are the answers spoiler-free?

Hints and answers are hidden by default. Nothing is revealed until you tap, so you can read the introduction and strategy sections safely.

Do all of these puzzles reset at the same time?

Wordle, Connections, Strands and Contexto reset at midnight in your local time zone, while Spelling Bee releases a new hive each morning in Eastern Time. That means you may briefly see a different puzzle number than someone in another region.

How do I get better at word puzzles?

Learn the structure of each game, such as common letter patterns in Wordle or red herrings in Connections, and keep notes on what you miss. Our strategy sections are written to make tomorrow’s puzzle easier, not only today’s.
