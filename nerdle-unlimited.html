What is it?

The Free Online Nerdle Game

Nerdle is a math puzzle game that challenges you to guess a hidden arithmetic equation in six attempts. Unlike word games, every guess must be a valid equation — numbers, operators, and an equals sign, all arranged so the left side equals the right side.

Our free Nerdle Unlimited generates a fresh 8-character equation each game using standard arithmetic. The tiles turn green, purple, or gray to guide you — exactly like Wordle but for math. There's no daily limit, so you can play as many rounds as you want.

Whether you're a math enthusiast, a student practicing arithmetic, or just someone who enjoys a satisfying brain teaser, this online Nerdle is perfect for you. The game follows standard order of operations (BODMAS/PEMDAS), so multiplication and division come before addition and subtraction.

No signup, no downloads, no ads blocking your view — just a clean, beautiful math equation game that runs right in your browser. Track your wins and streaks, toggle dark mode, and enjoy endless number-cracking fun.
