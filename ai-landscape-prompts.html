Anatomy of a Perfect Landscapes Prompt

Every prompt above follows the same simple structure. Use this formula to write your own from scratch:

The formula

Location + time of day + weather + foreground / midground / background + lens + photography style

Write it as one natural sentence or a comma-separated list. Put the most important words first — most generators weigh the beginning of a prompt more heavily.

3 Pro Tips for Better Landscapes Results

1

Layer your depth

Mention a foreground element, a midground and a distant background for natural depth.

2

Weather sells mood

Mist, storms and snow add far more drama than clear blue skies.

3

Match ratio to use

Use 16:9 for desktop wallpapers and 9:16 for phone lock screens.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Writing only "beautiful mountain" — without light and weather the scene looks flat.
  • Writing very short prompts — details like light, lens and materials make the difference.
  • Using contradictory words (for example “minimalist” and “highly cluttered”).
  • Changing everything at once — adjust one element per run so you learn what works.
  • Not saving good prompts — keep winners in a notepad so you can reuse them.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make AI landscapes look real?

Reference real photography terms: lens focal length, long exposure, time of day, and natural weather.

What aspect ratio is best for wallpapers?

16:9 for desktop, 9:16 for phones, and 21:9 for ultrawide monitors.

Can I sell AI landscape prints?

Often yes, but rules vary by tool and platform. Read the licensing terms of your generator and print-on-demand service.
