Patterns to Look For

When you are a few words short of your goal, patterns beat brute force. Today’s hive is rich in structure, and these are the families to try.

  • Math and measurement: words built from MONO-, MINI- and -AL endings, such as the family around NOMINAL and MINIMAL.
  • Animals and plants: look at LAMA, LLAMA, LAMB and BAMBOO.
  • Doubled M words: MAMA, MAMMA, MOMMA and MAMMAL share a pattern. If one works, test the others.
  • Plurals and variants: IAMB and IAMBI, MINIM and MINIMA, and LAMINA and LAMINAL are close pairs. When you find one, check its relatives.
  • Words ending in -ION: MINION and MILLION use I and O together, a combination that appears in several answers.

The fastest way to finish a hive

Work through the alphabet systematically. Take each outer letter in turn and try it with the center letter in every position. Then double each letter. This method sounds slow, but it finds words your instinct skips, particularly short ones such as LIMN and MOIL.

Hive Strategy for Any Day

1

Shuffle often

The shuffle button is free. Rearranging the outer letters breaks the mental pattern you have been stuck in and often reveals a word you could not see before.

2

Find the pangram early

A pangram gives a large bonus and tells you which prefixes and suffixes the hive supports. Look for common endings first, such as -ING, -ED, -AL and -ION.

3

Use the hints grid

The in-app hints show how many words start with each pair of letters. Compare those counts with what you have found to see which prefix still has gaps.

4

Respect the word list

If a word is rejected, move on. The word list excludes many proper nouns and rare words, so insisting on one rarely pays off.

Spelling Bee FAQ

What are today's Spelling Bee letters?

The center letter is M, and the six outer letters are A, B, I, L, N and O. Every answer must contain the M.

What are the pangrams for today's Spelling Bee?

There are two pangrams for October 25, 2026: BINOMIAL (8 letters) and BIBLIOMANIA (11 letters). Each uses all seven letters and earns a 7-point bonus.

How many words are in today's Spelling Bee?

The puzzle accepts 66 words worth 282 points in total, including the two pangrams.

How many points do I need for Genius today?

Genius is 70% of the total points, which comes to about 197 points on a 282-point board. Queen Bee requires finding every word.

Can I reuse letters in Spelling Bee?

Yes. Letters can repeat as often as you like, which is why words like MAMMA and MONOMANIA are valid. Words must be at least four letters long and must include the center letter.

Why was my word rejected?

The puzzle uses a curated word list that excludes many proper nouns, hyphenated words, obscure terms and offensive words. A real word can still be rejected if it is not on that list.

When does the Spelling Bee reset?

A new hive unlocks daily at 3 a.m. Eastern Time, so the answer list for yesterday’s puzzle is shown in the app the next morning.
