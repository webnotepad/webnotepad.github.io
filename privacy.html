Privacy Policy

At WebNotePad, we believe that your thoughts should remain yours. Our privacy philosophy is simple: we don't want your personal notes or diary entries, so we don't collect them. This Privacy Policy explains what limited information may be collected when you visit our website, how advertising partners operate, and your choices regarding cookies and personalized ads.

1. Data Collection

WebNotePad does not require an account, email address, or any personal identification to use our tools. We do not collect, store, or have access to the content of the notes, diary entries, or other text you create. Your notes and journal entries are stored entirely within your own browser's Local Storage.

2. Local Storage

Your notes and diary entries are saved locally on your device. This means:

  • Notes and diary content never leave your computer or phone.
  • We cannot "see," access, or recover your notes if you clear your browser data or lose your device.
  • Your data is not synced across different browsers or devices unless you manually export and import it.
  • Clearing site data, cookies, or local storage for this website will permanently delete your saved notes and entries from that browser.

3. Analytics

We may use minimal, privacy-focused analytics tools to understand aggregate website traffic and improve performance (for example, which pages are visited most often). These tools are configured to avoid collecting Personally Identifiable Information (PII) where possible. They may collect standard technical information such as approximate location (country/city level), browser type, device type, and referring pages.

4. Advertising, Cookies & Third-Party Services

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website and/or other websites on the Internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can visit www.aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising.

When ads are served, the following types of information may be collected by Google or other advertising partners through cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited and interactions on our site
  • Referring website
  • Approximate geographic location

This information is used to deliver relevant ads, measure ad performance, detect and prevent fraud, and improve advertising services. We do not combine this information with the content of your notes or diary entries (which remain local to your device).

For more details about how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps, please see How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps and Google's full Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to other sites. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. These external sites are not operated by us, and we have no control over their content or privacy practices. We advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.

6. Children's Privacy

WebNotePad is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or for other operational reasons. We will post the updated policy on this page and update the "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
