Questions

Bionic Reading FAQ.

Bionic Reading is a reading technique where the first few letters of each word are bolded, creating artificial fixation points that guide your eyes through the text more efficiently. The idea is that your brain completes the rest of each word from memory, allowing you to read faster and with better concentration.

Many readers report improved focus and faster reading with Bionic Reading, especially those with ADHD or dyslexia. Scientific evidence is still emerging, and results vary from person to person. It is best treated as an optional aid — try it and see whether it works for you.
