Patterns to Look For

When you are a few words short of your goal, patterns beat brute force. Today’s hive is rich in structure, and these are the families to try.

  • -ING participles: Look at LIGHTING, TINGLING, GIGGLING, and HIGHLIGHTING which build directly off shorter base words.
  • -LY modifiers: Words like TIGHTLY, THINLY, and LIGHTLY share structural suffixes.
  • Double consonants: G and L create dense clusters like GIGGLY and NIGGLY.

The fastest way to finish a hive

Work through the alphabet systematically. Take each outer letter in turn and try it with the center letter in every position. Then double each letter.

Spelling Bee FAQ

What are today's Spelling Bee letters?

What are the pangrams for today's Spelling Bee?

How many words are in today's Spelling Bee?

The puzzle accepts 43 words worth 245 points in total, including the pangram.

How many points do I need for Genius today?

Genius is 70% of the total points, which comes to about 172 points on a 245-point board.

Can I reuse letters in Spelling Bee?

Yes. Letters can repeat as often as you like. Words must be at least four letters long and must include the center letter.

Why was my word rejected?

The puzzle uses a curated word list that excludes many proper nouns, hyphenated words, obscure terms and offensive words.
