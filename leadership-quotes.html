Leadership Quotes for Influence

On trust, courage, listening and the quiet work of making other people better. Ten leadership quotes you can copy in one tap.

Leadership is mostly unglamorous. It is the listening you do before a decision, the credit you give away afterwards, and the consistency that makes people trust you on an ordinary Tuesday. These quotes are chosen for that work.

Leadership Quotes

“

A leader is one who knows the way, goes the way, and shows the way.

John C. Maxwell
“

Leadership is not about being in charge. It is about taking care of those in your charge.

Simon Sinek
“

The greatest leader is not necessarily the one who does the greatest things. He is the one that gets the people to do the greatest things.

Ronald Reagan
“

If your actions inspire others to dream more, learn more, do more and become more, you are a leader.

John Quincy Adams
“

It is amazing what you can accomplish if you do not care who gets the credit.

Harry S. Truman
“

To handle yourself, use your head; to handle others, use your heart.

Eleanor Roosevelt
“

The function of leadership is to produce more leaders, not more followers.

Ralph Nader
“

A genuine leader is not a searcher for consensus but a molder of consensus.

Martin Luther King Jr.
“

The best executive is the one who has sense enough to pick good people to do what he wants done, and self-restraint to keep from meddling with them while they do it.

Theodore Roosevelt
“

Courage is what it takes to stand up and speak; courage is also what it takes to sit down and listen.

Winston Churchill

How to Use These Quotes

Leadership quotes are most useful as a checklist, not a decoration. Take the one that makes you uncomfortable, then write one honest sentence about where you fall short of it. Keep that sentence somewhere you will read it before your next one-to-one. A meeting notes template is a good place to start.

The test of a leader is not the speech they give but the behaviour people copy when they are not in the room.— WebNotePad Team
