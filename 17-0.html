What is it?

The Free 17-0 Roster Builder

17-0 is a free NFL roster-building game built around one question: can you draft a squad good enough to go undefeated? You fill nine roster slots — quarterback, running back, two wide receivers, tight end, offensive line, defensive line, linebacker and defensive back — one pick at a time.

For each slot you are offered three players from random teams, seasons and eras. Some are legends, some are solid starters, and some are traps. Pick the best fit, then move on. When your roster is set, a season simulation plays out a full 17-game schedule against modelled opponents to see how you fare.

The draft has real depth. The quarterback carries the most weight in your overall rating, followed by your skill positions and defense. Players from the same team or era earn a chemistry bonus, so stacking a historic squad can be a winning strategy — but it is risky if the offers dry up.

When the season ends you get a full breakdown: week-by-week results, final record, playoff outcome, team rating, chemistry score and an Undefeated badge if you run the table. Then share a spoiler-free emoji grid with friends and try to beat your best draft.

17-0 is a free NFL roster-building game. You draft one player at a time from random teams, seasons and eras to fill nine roster slots. A rating engine then simulates a full 17-game season to see whether your squad can finish undefeated, reach the playoffs, or go all the way.

Press Start Draft. For each of the nine roster slots you are offered three players from random teams and eras. Pick one per slot to build your roster. When all nine slots are filled, press Simulate Season to run the 17-game schedule and see how your team performs.

The nine slots are QB (quarterback), RB (running back), WR1 and WR2 (wide receivers), TE (tight end), OL (offensive line), DL (defensive line), LB (linebacker) and DB (defensive back). Each slot has its own weighting, with QB contributing the most to your overall team rating.

If your drafted players come from the same NFL team or the same era, you earn a chemistry bonus that boosts your overall team rating. Stacking players from one historic squad or one decade is a risky but rewarding strategy.

Your final team rating is compared week by week against a simulated schedule of 17 opponents. Each week's result is computed from your rating, the opponent's strength and a small amount of randomness. The simulation then shows your record, playoff results, and whether you achieved a perfect 17-0 season.
