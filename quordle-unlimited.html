How it works

Play Quordle in 3 simple steps.

Guess a 5-Letter Word

Type any valid 5-letter word and press Enter. Your guess is applied to all four boards simultaneously. You have 9 attempts to solve all four words.

02

Read the Color Clues

🟩 Green means the letter is correct and in the right spot for that board. 🟨 Yellow means the letter is in that word but in a different position. ⬛ Gray means the letter is not in that word.

What is it?

The Free Online Quordle Game

Quordle is a word puzzle that challenges you to guess four 5-letter words simultaneously in nine attempts. Every guess you make is applied to all four boards, and the color-coded feedback helps you narrow down the possibilities for each word independently.

Our free Quordle Unlimited includes a comprehensive list of common five-letter English words. The four target words are randomly selected each game, so you get a fresh challenge every time you play. There's no daily limit — play as many games as you like.

Whether you're a word game enthusiast, a student looking to improve your vocabulary, or just someone who enjoys a good brain teaser, this online Quordle is perfect for you. The game is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktop. Use the on-screen keyboard or your physical keyboard to enter guesses.

No signup, no downloads, no ads blocking your view — just a clean, beautiful multi-word puzzle game that runs right in your browser. Track your wins and streaks, toggle dark mode, and enjoy endless word-solving fun.
