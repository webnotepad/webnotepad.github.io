Strategy

Tips to solve
Wordle faster.

Use these proven strategies alongside the solver to crack the puzzle in fewer guesses.

🧠 Opening Words

  • CRANE — covers C, R, A, N, E (three of the most common letters).
  • SLATE — hits S, L, A, T, E, including two top-5 consonants.
  • AUDIO — locks in four vowels and D in one guess.
  • RAISE — strong vowel coverage with R and S.

🎯 Reading the Colors

  • 🟩 Green: letter is correct and in the right spot.
  • 🟨 Yellow: letter is in the word but in a different position.
  • ⬛ Gray: letter is not in the word at all.
  • Repeated letters: each tile is independent — if one is gray and another green, the letter appears exactly once.

📊 Letter Frequency

  • Most common vowels: E, A, O, I, U.
  • Most common consonants: R, S, T, L, N, C, D, H.
  • Words ending in -ER, -LY, -ED, -TION are common.
  • Avoid repeating letters in early guesses unless you're confident.

🔄 Using the Solver

  • After each guess, update the solver with the new color information.
  • If the list is too long, make a strategic guess that eliminates many possibilities.
  • Sort mentally by common word patterns and try the most likely answer first.
  • Use the solver to check if a word you have in mind is still possible.
