Farming Guide

Plan Smarter, Spend Less.

Ascending a character is mostly a scheduling problem. Mora is the long-term limit, weekly boss materials are the slowest gate, and talent books can only be farmed on certain weekdays. A good plan keeps all three moving at once.

Priorities that save time

  • Level and ascend to 80 first. The final 80 to 90 stretch costs about 41% of all EXP, so delay it until the character is part of your main team.
  • Raise the talents you actually use. Skill and Burst often matter more than Normal Attack for support and reaction characters.
  • Weekly boss materials gate talents 7 to 10. Plan around the three discounted runs per week.
  • Talent books need ascension phase 2 or higher, so unlock phase 2 before stockpiling books.
  • Use Sundays for the series you need most, since every domain is open.

Adventure Rank gates

Phases 1 to 6 need Adventure Rank 15, 25, 30, 35, 40 and 50. The planner shows the AR your target requires so you can see when a plan is realistic.
