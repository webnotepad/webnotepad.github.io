What is it?

The Free Online Letter Boxed Game

Letter Boxed is a word puzzle where 12 letters are arranged around a square. You form words by connecting letters, but consecutive letters must come from different sides of the box — no two letters from the same side can be adjacent in a word.

Our free Letter Boxed Unlimited generates a fresh set of 12 letters each game. Every word you submit must be a valid English word of at least 3 letters, and the last letter of one word must become the first letter of the next. The goal is to use all 12 letters in as few words as possible.

Whether you're a word game enthusiast, a student looking to expand your vocabulary, or just someone who enjoys a good mental challenge, this online Letter Boxed is perfect for you. The game is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktop. Tap the letters on screen or use your keyboard to type.

No signup, no downloads, no ads blocking your view — just a clean, beautiful word connection game that runs right in your browser. Track your wins and streaks, toggle dark mode, and enjoy endless letter-connecting fun.
