A Syllable Counter is a tool that counts how many syllables are in a word, line, or entire block of text. It is commonly used by poets writing haikus or sonnets, songwriters matching lyrics to melodies, and students learning English pronunciation.

The counter uses a heuristic based on vowel groups. Each contiguous group of vowels (a, e, i, o, u, and sometimes y) counts as one syllable, with adjustments for silent-e endings and common English word patterns. It runs entirely in your browser.
