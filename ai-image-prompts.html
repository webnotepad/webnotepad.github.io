Why a Good AI Prompt Matters

An AI image generator is only as good as the instructions it receives. A strong prompt names the subject, the lighting, the camera or art style and the mood. Our library gives you proven starting points so you can skip the trial and error and focus on creating.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are AI image prompts?

AI image prompts are text descriptions you give to image generators such as Midjourney, DALL·E or Stable Diffusion. The more specific the prompt — subject, lighting, style and camera — the better the image.

Are these prompts free to use?

Yes. Every prompt in this library is free to copy and modify. Commercial usage rights for the images you generate depend on the AI tool you use.

Which AI generators do these prompts work with?

They work with Midjourney, DALL·E 3, Leonardo AI, Flux, Stable Diffusion and most other text-to-image tools.

How do I copy a prompt?

Click any image to open a popup with the full prompt, then press the Copy Prompt button.
