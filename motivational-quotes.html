Motivation is not a mood you wait for. It is usually the result of a small action taken before you feel ready. These quotes are chosen for that specific job: to shorten the distance between thinking about the work and doing it.

How to Use These Quotes

Pick one quote for the week, not ten. Copy it into a free online notepad or diary entry, and write one sentence back to it: "This week I will…" That reply is what turns an inspirational line into a plan.

Motivation follows action more often than it precedes it. Start small enough that starting is easy.— WebNotePad Team
