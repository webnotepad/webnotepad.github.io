Understanding Energy Recharge

How Energy Recharge Works.

Elemental Bursts need energy, and most of that energy comes from Elemental Particles and Orbs dropped by skills and enemies. Energy Recharge, or ER, is a multiplier on that particle energy. At 150% ER, a particle worth 3 energy gives 4.5.

How much each particle is worth depends on its element, whether your character is on the field, and how many characters are in your party. Matching element particles are worth three times as much as other elements, which is why batteries of the same element are so valuable.

Energy Recharge does not change flat energy, such as energy gained from some passives. This calculator keeps the two separate and solves for the ER you need to afford your burst every rotation.

ER Guide

Build the Right Amount of ER.

More ER is not always better. Too little leaves your burst unavailable, and too much wastes substats that could add damage.

Tips

  • Use the exact number of particles your team produces in one rotation, not a guess. Count every skill and enemy drop.
  • Favonius weapons help the whole team, but the number of procs depends on crit rate and cooldown, so treat the estimate as an average.
  • If you cast your burst every second rotation, set rotations per burst to 2 and the requirement drops.
  • Pick up particles with the right character. On-field matching-element particles are worth the most.
