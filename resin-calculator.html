Understanding Resin

How Original Resin Works.

Original Resin is the stamina currency of Genshin Impact. You spend it to claim rewards from domains, ley lines, bosses and weekly challenges. It recharges on its own at one point every eight minutes, which is 7.5 per hour and 180 per day.

Since Version 4.7 the cap is 200, so an empty bar takes 26 hours and 40 minutes to fill. Once you reach the cap, natural recharge pauses, so any time spent at 200 is wasted progress. That is why this calculator shows both the time to your target and the time to the cap.

If you need resin sooner, you can restore 60 at a time with Fragile Resin or by spending Primogems, up to six refreshes a day at rising prices. Plan ahead and you rarely need to pay.

Resin Guide

Spend Smarter, Waste Less.

Good resin habits come down to never sitting at the cap and spending on the highest-value activities first.

Priorities

  • Claim your three discounted weekly boss rewards each week before spending elsewhere, since they cost 30 instead of 60.
  • Use Condensed Resin on domains and ley lines for double rewards, and craft it when you will be offline for a long time.
  • Check the “time to cap” figure before bed or work, and spend enough resin to avoid waste.
  • Primogem refreshes are the last resort. The first refresh costs about 0.83 Primogems per resin, and the last two cost 3.33.
