The Math

Genshin Damage Formula.

Base Damage

Talent % × Scaling Stat

Scaling stat is usually ATK, but can be HP, DEF or Elemental Mastery. Total ATK = (character + weapon base) × (1 + ATK%) + flat ATK.

Multipliers

Base × (1 + DMG%) × Crit × DEF × RES

Elemental and other DMG bonuses add together. Crit multiplies by (1 + Crit DMG). Average uses Crit Rate capped at 100%.

Enemy Defense

(Lvl+100) ÷ ((Lvl+100) + (EnemyLvl+100) × (1−DEF Red))

Higher enemy level means more DEF. DEF reduction buffs push this multiplier closer to 1.

Build Guide

Crit Ratio & Stat Priority.

Crit Rate and Crit DMG work best together. For a fixed number of crit substat rolls, a roughly 1:2 ratio (for example 60% / 120%) gives the highest average damage. If your Crit Rate is already past 100%, extra rate is wasted, so switch rolls into Crit DMG, ATK% or Elemental Mastery.

Quick priorities

  • Reaction teams (Vaporize, Melt, Aggravate): Elemental Mastery grows quickly at low values and tapers off.
  • Pure DPS characters: ATK%, Crit Rate and Crit DMG, plus the matching elemental DMG goblet.
  • HP or DEF scalers: replace ATK% with HP% or DEF%, then check the Stat Gain Comparison panel.
  • Enemy RES shred and DEF reduction act as multipliers on all your damage, which is why support buffs are so strong.
