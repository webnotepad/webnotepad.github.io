What is it?

The Free Animated Greeting Card Maker

Season Greeting Card Maker is a free browser-based tool for building animated greeting cards. Pick a seasonal theme, add a recipient name, your name and a custom wish, then share the card as a live link or download it as a PNG.

Each theme is alive with canvas particle effects — snow drifting down for winter, leaves tumbling for autumn, petals floating for spring, and confetti bursting for celebrations. The particles run in the live preview and in the shared card, so the card feels animated the moment it is opened.

The wax-seal envelope reveal adds a moment of ceremony. Shared cards arrive sealed; the recipient taps the wax stamp and the envelope opens to reveal the message inside. It is a small interaction that makes a digital card feel like a gift.

Because everything lives in the URL hash, there is no backend, no account and no database. You can share a link, download a PNG, or both — and every card includes a gentle prompt to send one of your own, keeping the wishes moving.
