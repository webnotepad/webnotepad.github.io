What is it?

The Free Spelling Bee Unlimited Game

Spelling Bee Unlimited is a free online word game inspired by the NYT Spelling Bee. Each puzzle gives you a honeycomb hive of seven letters. Your goal is to find as many valid English words as you can, with two rules: every word must be at least four letters long, and every word must include the centre letter of the hive. Letters can be reused as many times as you like.

What sets Spelling Bee Unlimited apart is that there is no daily limit. The original game releases one puzzle per day; here, every press of the New Puzzle button instantly loads a fresh hive from a large word database. You can binge puzzles, take your time on a hard one, or use the game as a warm-up before the daily. No two games feel the same.

Points are awarded for every valid word, with longer words worth more. Any word that uses all seven letters is a pangram and earns a substantial bonus — often the difference between reaching Genius and Queen Bee. Your total score also determines your rank, which climbs from Beginner all the way to Queen Bee as you find more and more words.

When you get stuck, a Hint button reveals the first two letters of an unfound word, giving you a nudge without spoiling the answer. Your found words are listed on the side with their point values, and hints you have used are tracked separately. Everything happens locally in your browser — nothing is sent to our servers, and your best scores are stored only on your device.
