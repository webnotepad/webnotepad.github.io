How to Find a Spangram Faster

Many players hunt for the spangram first, and there is good reason to. Once it is found, it tells you the theme in plain language, and it removes a long string of letters from the board.

1

Read the title twice

The spangram is almost always a more specific version or pun on the title.

2

Look at the edges

A spangram must touch two opposite sides of the board.

3

Use the length hint

Note the expected length when hints are active.

4

Search for common endings

Endings or compound word joins can point you to the correct path across the grid.

A Daily Strands Playbook

The rules of Strands are simple, but a few habits will help every puzzle. Strands words must be at least four letters long, you can connect letters in any direction, and every three valid non-theme words you find earns a hint.

  1. Hunt for non-theme words early. Three short words earn a free hint.
  2. Keep hints for the end. Use them when you are stuck on the last one or two words.
  3. Try the opening letters. Scan the grid for known initial pairs.
  4. Check leftover letters. Ensure all board tiles are accounted for.

Strands FAQ

What is today's NYT Strands spangram?

The spangram for NYT Strands #967 on October 26, 2026 is WRAPMUSIC. It has nine letters and describes things that are wrapped.

What is the theme of NYT Strands today?

The theme is ‘That's a wrap’. Every theme word is something literal or figurative that gets wrapped up.

What are today's NYT Strands answers?

Besides the spangram WRAPMUSIC, the five theme words are GIFT, MOVIE, BURRITO, LEFTOVERS and TURBAN, for six answers in total.

How many theme words are in today's Strands?

There are six, counting the spangram.

How do Strands hints work?

Every three valid non-theme words you find, at least four letters long, earns a hint that highlights the letters of one theme word.

What is a spangram in Strands?

A spangram is a special theme answer that describes the puzzle’s topic and stretches from one side of the board to the opposite side.

Do Strands words have to be in a straight line?

No. You can connect adjacent letters in any direction, including diagonally, as long as each letter tile is used only once in a word.
