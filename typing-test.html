What is it?

The Free Online Typing Speed Test

A typing test measures how fast and accurately you can type on a keyboard. It reports your speed in words per minute (WPM) and your accuracy as a percentage. Our live typing test gives you real-time feedback while you type, so you always know exactly how you're doing.

The WPM standard used across all typing websites is simple: one "word" equals five characters, including spaces. If you type 250 correct characters in one minute, that's 50 WPM. Our words per minute test uses this exact formula, so your results are directly comparable to any other typing test online.

Regular typing practise is the fastest way to improve. Use the "New Text" button to load fresh passages and treat every test as a typing exercise. Over a few weeks of short daily sessions, most people see their WPM increase by 10–20. Our keyboard speed test works with any physical keyboard — desktop, laptop, or a tablet with an external keyboard.

Everything runs locally in your browser. No signup, no stored scores, no tracking — just a clean, private writing speed test you can take as often as you like.
