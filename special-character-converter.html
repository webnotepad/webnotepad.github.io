What is it?

The Complete Special Character Converter

A special character converter is an essential tool for anyone working with international text. Different languages use accented characters and special letters that, while beautiful in their native scripts, can cause problems in file names, URLs, databases, and legacy systems that only support plain ASCII. This tool bridges that gap by intelligently converting special characters to their closest ASCII equivalents.

Our converter offers four powerful modes. German Umlauts handles the classic German transliteration rules: ä becomes ae, ö becomes oe, ü becomes ue, and ß becomes ss. This is the standard method used in Germany when umlauts aren't available. Nordic Letters handles Scandinavian characters like å (aa), ø (oe), and æ (ae). Diacritic Stripper removes all accent marks from Latin characters — é becomes e, ñ becomes n, ç becomes c, and hundreds more. Finally, SEO URL Slugify combines all conversions and adds URL-safe formatting: spaces become hyphens, special characters are removed, and everything is lowercased.

Like all WebNotePad tools, this converter runs entirely in your browser. Your text never leaves your device — no server uploads, no tracking, no privacy concerns. It's perfect for web developers creating clean URLs, data scientists normalizing datasets, content managers preparing SEO-friendly slugs, or anyone who needs to convert text for systems that don't support Unicode.
