How to Enter Cheats in GTA 6

There’s no number-dialing in GTA 6. Cheats use D-pad sequences that you enter through your in-game phone, with two shoulder buttons held. The arrows are identical on PS5 and Xbox.

PS5

D-pad ↑ opens phone
Hold L1 + R1, then enter the code

Xbox Series X|S

D-pad ↑ opens phone
Hold LB + RB, then enter the code
1

Finish the main story

This unlocks a Cheats section under the Progress tab in the main menu. Nothing works before this.

2

Meet the cheat’s own requirement

Most cheats also need a challenge, such as shooting-range medals, workouts or collectibles. Knowing a code won’t bypass it.

3

Bring out your phone in free roam

Press Up on the D-pad.

4

Hold the shoulder buttons and enter the code

Keep L1+R1 (PS5) or LB+RB (Xbox) held while pressing the arrows in order.

Quick FAQ

Do cheats work in GTA 6 Online?

No. Cheats are a single-player feature. They are not available in any online or multiplayer mode, so don’t expect these codes to do anything there.

Do cheats disable Trophies and Achievements in GTA 6?

Yes. Activating any cheat disables saving and prevents you from earning Trophies (PS5) or Achievements (Xbox Series X|S) for the rest of that play session. Restart the session to earn progress again.

Can I use GTA 6 cheats from the start of the game?

No. You must finish the main story first, which unlocks a Cheats section under the Progress tab in the main menu. Most cheats also have their own unlock challenge, so knowing a code in advance won’t bypass it.

How do I enter cheat codes on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S?

In free roam, press Up on the D-pad to bring out your phone. Hold L1 and R1 on PS5, or LB and RB on Xbox, then enter the D-pad sequence while still holding both buttons.

Is there a GTA 6 money cheat?

Yes. The $250,000 cheat unlocks when you finish the main story. The code is Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right.

Why isn’t my cheat code working?

Most often the cheat hasn’t been unlocked yet, you’re entering it without holding L1+R1 (PS5) or LB+RB (Xbox), or the code is one that hasn’t been discovered yet. Double-check the unlock requirement in the tables above.

Are there GTA 6 cheats for PC?

GTA 6 launched on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, so there are no PC cheats at this time. Be careful with sites listing text-style “PC cheat words” for GTA 6; these aren’t confirmed by launch guides.

Are there cheats for max health or invincibility in GTA 6?

Not in the 61 cheats reported so far. The player cheats cover things like Body Armor, Unlimited Focus, No Fall Damage and wanted-level changes. This page is updated as new codes are found.
