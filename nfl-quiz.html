What is it?

The Free NFL Quiz Game

NFL Quiz is a free online trivia game that puts your football knowledge to the test. Each round serves up 10 multiple-choice questions covering the National Football League — its teams, players, rules, statistics and history. Pick the right answer from four options before the 20-second clock runs out.

Whether you're a lifelong fan who knows every Super Bowl score or a casual viewer who just wants to settle a debate, the NFL Quiz is built to be fast, fair and fun. Questions range from easy warm-ups (how many points is a touchdown?) to deep-cut trivia (who was the first player to rush for 2,000 yards in a season?).

Choose a category filter before each round to focus on what you care about, or leave it on All for a mixed challenge. Every answer comes with instant feedback and a short explanation, so you walk away knowing a little more than when you started.

Your best score and total rounds played are stored locally in your browser. When you finish a round, you can share a spoiler-free emoji grid with friends — the same satisfying share you know from Wordle, but for football. No signup, no download, no daily limit.
