    What it does

    A fast, private proofreading pass for everything you write

    A grammar and spelling checker is the difference between a message that lands and one that gets quietly ignored. Our checker scans your text for the mistakes that slip past tired eyes: misspelled words, repeated words, missing spaces after punctuation, lowercase sentence starts, could of / should of constructions, your vs. you’re and its vs. it’s confusions, a vs. an article errors and repeated punctuation.

    Alongside correctness, the checker also flags wordy phrasing — phrases like “in order to”, “due to the fact that” and “at this point in time” — so your writing reads tighter and more confidently.

    Everything runs locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device, never touches a server and is never stored anywhere. That makes it safe for private emails, unpublished drafts, client documents and anything else you would rather keep to yourself.
