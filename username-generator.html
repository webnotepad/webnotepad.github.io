What it does

A fast, private name machine for every platform

A username is the first thing people read about you online. Our generator combines two curated word banks per style to build usernames that are unique, readable and on-brand — then layers on optional numbers, leetspeak substitutions, decorative symbols and stylish Unicode fonts so you can dial the vibe up or down.

Every generated name respects your max length limit, and the optional no-duplicates switch keeps each batch clean. Save your favorites with a single click, copy any name to your clipboard instantly, or download the whole batch as a TXT file for later.

Because everything runs locally in your browser, nothing you type or generate is ever sent to a server. No accounts, no tracking, no uploads — just an endless stream of good names.
