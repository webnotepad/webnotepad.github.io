How it works

Play 2048 in 3 simple steps.

01

Slide the Tiles

Use the arrow keys on your keyboard or swipe on a touchscreen. All tiles slide in the direction you choose until they hit a wall or another tile.

02

Combine Matching Numbers

When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into a single tile with double the value. Each merge adds to your score. A new tile appears after every move.

03

Reach 2048

Keep combining tiles to create bigger numbers. Your goal is to build the 2048 tile. The game ends when no moves are possible — but you can undo one move at a time.

What is it?

The Free Online 2048 Puzzle Game

2048 is a deceptively simple sliding-tile puzzle created by Gabriele Cirulli in 2014. It quickly became a viral sensation, and for good reason: the rules take seconds to learn, but mastering the game requires real strategy, patience, and forward planning.

Our free online 2048 game stays true to the original while adding a beautiful, modern interface. The board uses the classic warm palette with smooth tile animations, so every merge feels satisfying. It runs entirely in your browser — no downloads, no signup, no ads interrupting your flow.

You can play with the arrow keys on any desktop or laptop, or use swipe gestures on a phone or tablet. The game automatically saves your best score in your browser's local storage, so you can always try to beat your personal record. And if you make a mistake, the Undo button lets you take back your last move — a helpful feature for learning the game's deeper strategies.

Whether you're a puzzle veteran chasing a 4096 tile or a first-time player just trying to reach 2048, this browser-based puzzle game is a perfect way to take a mindful break, sharpen your focus, and enjoy a classic that has stood the test of time.
