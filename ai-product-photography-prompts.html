Anatomy of a Perfect Product Photography Prompt

Every prompt above follows the same simple structure. Use this formula to write your own from scratch:

The formula

Product + material + surface + background + lighting setup + camera angle + brand mood + "commercial photography"

Write it as one natural sentence or a comma-separated list. Put the most important words first — most generators weigh the beginning of a prompt more heavily.

3 Pro Tips for Better Product Photography Results

1

Specify the surface

The surface (marble, wood, glass) sets the whole brand feel and creates natural reflections.

2

Use "commercial photography"

This phrase pushes the model toward clean, ad-ready composition and lighting.

3

Leave copy space

Add "negative space on the right" if you plan to place text over the image.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Expecting readable label text — generate the scene first and add real labels afterwards.
  • Writing very short prompts — details like light, lens and materials make the difference.
  • Using contradictory words (for example “minimalist” and “highly cluttered”).
  • Changing everything at once — adjust one element per run so you learn what works.
  • Not saving good prompts — keep winners in a notepad so you can reuse them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can AI product photos replace a photographer?

For concept art, ads and mockups, often yes. For exact product accuracy, composite your real product photo onto an AI background.

How do I keep my logo accurate?

AI struggles with text. Generate the scene without the logo, then add your real logo in an editor.

What ratio is best for e-commerce?

1:1 for marketplace listings, 4:5 for Instagram, and 16:9 for web banners.
