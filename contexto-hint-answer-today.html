Contexto is unlike most word games. There is no grid, no color-coded letters and no limit on attempts. Instead, you type any word you like, and the game tells you how semantically close it is to the secret word. Spelling does not matter at all, only meaning and usage.

How Contexto Ranks Words

Contexto compares your guess with the secret word using a language model trained on large amounts of text. Every word gets a position in a sorted list, and the secret word sits at number 1. The ranking reflects how often two words appear in similar contexts, not whether they mean the same thing.

That distinction explains most of the surprises. Related wild animals rank close to the answer because they are frequently mentioned in similar wildlife articles and stories. When a guess seems to rank too well or too poorly, remember that you are measuring usage, not definitions.

What this means for your guesses

  • Think about situations, not synonyms. Ask where the word is used and who says it.
  • Try related family members. Wild canines like wolf and coyote rank right next to the answer.
  • Adjust length after finding the category. Once you know it's a wild animal, testing 3-letter words like DOG or FOX helps pinpoint the answer quickly.

A Strategy That Finds the Secret Word Faster

1

Spend your first five guesses on breadth

Pick words from different families. Avoid guessing three similar foods in a row, because the information overlaps.

2

Chase the trend, not the single best word

If five guesses all land in yellow, they probably share a theme. Name that theme, then think of the word that would sit at its center.

3

Use the hint button sparingly

Contexto’s hint reveals a word that is closer than your best guess so far. Use it to unstick yourself, not to skip the thinking.

4

Work backward from the letters

When a hint tells you the length or first letter, test candidates in your head against the cluster. A short 3-letter animal in a wild canine cluster points straight to the solution.

Today’s game is a great example of central theme logic. A solver who finds WOLF, COYOTE, and BEAR all clustered together might ask: what short 3-letter animal fits in this wild animal cluster? That leads directly to the secret word.

Contexto FAQ

What is today's Contexto answer?

The Contexto answer for October 28, 2026 (game #1501) is FOX. It is tucked behind a tap in the reveal box so you can use hints first.

What are good Contexto hints for today?

The secret word is a concrete noun with three letters. It starts with F, ends with X and refers to a wild canine famous for cleverness and a bushy tail.

What are the closest words to today's Contexto answer?

Words in the same semantic neighborhood include BEAR, WOLF, COYOTE, SKUNK, BEAVER, DOG, MOOSE, EAGLE, HAWK and LION.

How does Contexto rank words?

Contexto uses a language model to rank every word by how similar its meaning and typical usage are to the secret word. The secret word is number 1, and the lower your guess number, the closer you are.

What do the Contexto colors mean?

Green marks words ranked roughly 1 to 300, yellow marks roughly 301 to 1,500, and red marks everything beyond that. Aim for green and use red guesses to rule out whole areas of meaning.

Is there a limit on guesses in Contexto?

No. Contexto allows unlimited guesses, and it also offers a hint button and a give-up option if you are stuck.

Why do Contexto rankings feel strange?

The rankings measure how words are used together in text, not dictionary definitions. A word that often appears alongside the secret word can rank above a true synonym.
