How it works

Play Dordle in 3 simple steps.

01

Guess a 5-Letter Word

Type any valid 5-letter word and press Enter. Your guess is applied to both boards simultaneously. You have 7 attempts to solve both words.

02

Read the Color Clues

🟩 Green means the letter is correct and in the right spot for that board. 🟨 Yellow means the letter is in that word but in a different position. ⬛ Gray means the letter is not in that word.

03

Solve Both Words

Use the color feedback to deduce each word. A board becomes locked once solved. Keep guessing to solve the remaining board before you run out of 7 attempts.

What is it?

The Free Online Dordle Game

Dordle is a word puzzle that challenges you to guess two 5-letter words simultaneously in seven attempts. Every guess you make is applied to both boards, and the color-coded feedback helps you narrow down the possibilities for each word independently.

Our free Dordle Unlimited includes a comprehensive list of common five-letter English words. The two target words are randomly selected each game, so you get a fresh challenge every time you play. There's no daily limit — play as many games as you like.

Whether you're a word game enthusiast, a student looking to improve your vocabulary, or just someone who enjoys a good brain teaser, this online Dordle is perfect for you. The game is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktop. Use the on-screen keyboard or your physical keyboard to enter guesses.

No signup, no downloads, no ads blocking your view — just a clean, beautiful double-word puzzle game that runs right in your browser. Track your wins and streaks, toggle dark mode, and enjoy endless word-solving fun.
