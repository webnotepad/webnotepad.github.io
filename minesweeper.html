What is it?

The Free Online Minesweeper

Minesweeper is one of the most-played computer games of all time. Bundled with Windows from 1990 onward, it became a worldwide phenomenon — a game of pure logic that anyone can learn in a minute but nobody ever truly masters. Our free Minesweeper brings the classic experience to the browser with all the quality-of-life improvements modern players expect.

The rules are simple: reveal all the safe squares on a rectangular grid without detonating a mine. Numbers that appear when you uncover a square tell you how many of the eight surrounding squares contain a mine. Using those numbers and careful deduction, you mark the mines with flags and clear the rest of the board. Clear every safe square and you win.

We build in several improvements over the original: first-click safety means you can never lose on your very first move, flag mode makes the game fully playable on mobile, and chord clicking lets you reveal neighbours with a single tap once you've placed the right flags. There is also a live timer, a mine counter and per-difficulty best times so you always know where you stand.

Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing is sent to our servers and no data is stored beyond your own best times. Play as many rounds as you like, at any difficulty, for as long as you like.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Minesweeper is a classic single-player logic puzzle. The goal is to clear a rectangular board containing hidden mines without detonating any of them. Numbers in revealed squares tell you how many mines are in the eight surrounding squares. Using these clues and careful deduction, you flag every mine and reveal every safe square to win.

Click or tap any square to reveal it. If it is a mine, you lose. If it is a number, it shows how many of its eight neighbours contain mines. If it is blank, all adjacent safe squares reveal automatically. Use the numbers to deduce where the mines are, then flag them with a right-click (or the Flag button on mobile). Clear every safe square to win.

First-click safety guarantees that the first square you click is never a mine and always opens a safe starting area. This means you can never lose on your very first move — the mines are only placed after you click. It is one of the quality-of-life improvements over the original Windows Minesweeper.

On desktop, right-click any hidden square to place or remove a flag. On mobile, tap the Flag button at the bottom of the board to toggle flag mode, then tap the squares you want to mark. Flagging helps you remember which squares you have identified as mines.

Chording is a shortcut: when you click a numbered square that already has exactly the right number of flags around it, all of its remaining hidden neighbours are revealed at once. It is faster than clicking each neighbour individually and is essential for advanced play.
