What is it?

The Free Online Word Search

A word search is a word puzzle where a list of themed words is hidden inside a grid of letters. The words can run horizontally, vertically, diagonally — and depending on difficulty, backwards too. Your job is to trace each hidden word through the grid and cross it off the list.

Our free word search game generates a brand-new puzzle every time you press the New Puzzle button, drawing from a large library of themed word sets. That means you can play as many puzzles as you want, at any difficulty, without ever seeing the same grid twice. No daily limits, no signups, no ads inside the game.

The game uses the classic eight-direction layout you know from puzzle books and newspapers: left-to-right and top-to-bottom on Easy, all four diagonals added on Medium, and full reversal on Hard. Words are always at least four letters long so grids feel tight and satisfying rather than sparse.

Play with a mouse on desktop — click and drag from the first letter to the last — or with your thumbs on mobile by tapping the first letter and then the last. Every puzzle gives you three hints if you get stuck, and the timer keeps track of how quickly you solve. Your best time per difficulty is saved in your browser so you always have something to beat.
