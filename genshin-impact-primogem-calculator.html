Wish Guide

Save Smarter, Wish Better.

Primogems are the only currency that matters for limited banners, so the best strategy is knowing your target before the banner starts. Pity carries over between banners of the same type, which means pulls you make now still count later.

Practical tips

  • Plan for the 90% figure, not the average. The average hides the unlucky half of outcomes.
  • Do not stop inside soft pity. Pulls from wish 74 onward are the most efficient ones you will ever make.
  • Fate Points on the weapon banner reset when it ends, so only start a weapon pull if you can finish it.
  • Spiral Abyss, Imaginarium Theater and Stygian Onslaught reset on fixed schedules, so clear them before the reset to avoid losing primogems.
  • Intertwined Fates from Paimon's Bargains count as 160 primogems each in this calculator.

How Capturing Radiance helps

On a non-guaranteed 5★ the calculator uses a 55% overall chance of the featured character, and forces a win after three consecutive losses, matching the published mechanic.

Frequently Asked Questions.

One wish costs 160 Primogems or one Intertwined Fate. Genesis Crystals convert to Primogems at 1:1.

A 5★ is guaranteed by wish 90 (14,400 Primogems). Guaranteeing the featured character from zero pity takes up to 180 wishes, or 28,800 Primogems.

Soft pity is when the 5★ rate rises sharply. It starts around wish 74 on character banners and around wish 63 on weapon banners, before hard pity at 90 and 80.

It can turn a lost 50/50 into a win, raising the overall featured chance to about 55%, and it is guaranteed after three consecutive 50/50 losses. It does not change the worst case of 180 wishes.

75% of weapon 5★ drops are featured. If your 5★ is not your chosen weapon you gain one Fate Point, and with one Fate Point your next 5★ is your chosen weapon. Worst case is 160 wishes, and Fate Points reset when the banner ends.

Recent patches have given roughly 9,000 to 14,000 Primogems over 42 days from dailies, Spiral Abyss, Imaginarium Theater, Stygian Onslaught, events and compensation. Welkin Moon adds about 3,780 more.
