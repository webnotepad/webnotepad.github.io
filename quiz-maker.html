How it works

Build a quiz in 3 steps.

01

Add Your Questions

Give your quiz a title and description, then press Add Question. Type the question and fill in four answer options. Mark the correct answer with the radio button next to it.

02

Preview & Refine

Press Preview Quiz at any time to run the quiz in a built-in player. Answer the questions, see instant feedback, and get a final score. Close the preview to keep editing.

03

Export & Share

When you are happy with the quiz, use Export JSON to copy or download it. You can later Import JSON to bring it back, back it up, or move it to another device.

What is it?

The Free Online Quiz Maker

Quiz Maker is a free browser-based tool for building multiple-choice quizzes. Teachers can create classroom quizzes, creators can build trivia rounds, and teams can draft training checks — all without signing up or installing anything.

You start with a title and description, then add questions one at a time. Each question has four answer options and a correct answer you mark with a single click. You can reorder questions, delete them, and keep editing until the quiz feels right.

The live preview lets you play your quiz as a learner would: answer each question, get instant feedback, and see a final score. It is the fastest way to catch a wrong answer or a confusing question before you share it.

When your quiz is ready, export it as JSON to save, back up or share it. The data model is deliberately simple, so the exported file works with the built-in importer and is easy to feed into your own quiz player. Everything is saved to your browser as you type, so you can close the tab and come back later.
