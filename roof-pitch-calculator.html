Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

Roof pitch describes the steepness of a roof. It's usually written as a ratio of rise over run, such as 4:12, meaning the roof rises 4 inches for every 12 inches of horizontal run. It can also be expressed as an angle in degrees.

To convert roof pitch (x:12) to degrees, use the formula: angle = arctan(x / 12). For example, a 6:12 pitch gives arctan(0.5) ≈ 26.57°. The calculator does this automatically.

Roof pitch depends on climate and roofing material. A 4:12 to 6:12 pitch is common for residential homes, while steeper pitches (8:12 and above) shed water and snow better. Flat roofs (under 2:12) require special waterproofing.

Rafter length can be calculated using the Pythagorean theorem: rafter = √(rise² + run²). For a 6:12 pitch with a 12-foot run: rise = 6 ft, rafter = √(36 + 144) = √180 ≈ 13.42 ft. Our calculator returns this automatically.

Slope percentage is rise divided by run, multiplied by 100. A 6:12 pitch has a rise/run of 0.5, so the slope is 50%. This is often used in civil engineering and drainage calculations.
