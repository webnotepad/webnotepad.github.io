What is it?

The Free Strong Password Generator

A password generator creates random, unpredictable strings that humans could never invent on their own. Our strong password generator uses your browser's built-in cryptographic random number generator, so every password is statistically independent and impossible to predict — even by us.

Most weak passwords fail for the same three reasons: they are too short, too predictable, or reused. A random password generator fixes all three at once. A 16-character password drawn from 94 possible characters has roughly 105 bits of entropy, which puts it far beyond the reach of brute-force attacks. Push it to 20 characters and you cross 130 bits.

Not every account needs a wall of symbols, though. For master passwords and Wi-Fi keys, a passphrase generator often works better: five random dictionary words are easier to remember and type than twelve random glyphs, while still delivering around 55 bits of entropy on their own. Our passphrase mode lets you add capitalisation and numbers for extra strength.

Every password you create here stays on your device. There is no server, no database and no log — the generator is pure client-side JavaScript. Use it as often as you like, for as many accounts as you have. Just remember the golden rule: one unique password per account, stored in a reputable password manager.
