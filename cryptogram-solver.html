Strategy

Tips to crack
any cryptogram.

Use these proven techniques alongside the solver to solve cryptograms faster.

📊 Letter Frequency

  • E is the most common English letter (~12.7%).
  • T is the most common consonant (~9.1%).
  • A, O, I, N all appear at 7–8% frequency.
  • The rarest letters are Z, Q, X, J — often appear only once or twice.
  • If a letter appears more than 10% of the time, it's almost certainly E.

🔤 Word Patterns

  • Single-letter words are almost always A or I.
  • The most common 3-letter word is THE.
  • Common 2-letter words: OF, TO, IN, IT, IS, BE, AS, AT, SO, WE, HE, BY, OR, ON, DO, IF, ME, MY, UP, AN, GO, NO, US, AM.
  • Common endings: -ING, -TION, -ED, -LY, -EST, -ER.
  • Repeated patterns like XYYX (e.g., "SEES", "THAT") are strong clues.

✏️ Work Iteratively

  • Start with the highest-frequency cipher letter — map it to E.
  • Find the most common 3-letter word and test THE.
  • Look for double letters — LL, EE, SS, OO, TT are most common.
  • Capitalized words in the middle of a sentence are usually proper nouns.
  • Use the frequency chart to cross-check your guesses.

🚫 Common Pitfalls

  • Don't assume the first guess is right — verify against multiple words.
  • Watch for letter-swaps like E↔T that are easy to confuse.
  • Punctuation doesn't always match English — some puzzle writers omit it.
  • Rare letters like Q are almost always followed by U.
  • If a mapping makes one word work but breaks another, undo it.
