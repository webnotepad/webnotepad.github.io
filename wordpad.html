What is it?

The Free Online WordPad Editor

WordPad Online is a free browser-based rich text editor that behaves like Microsoft WordPad, but runs entirely in your browser — no download, no install, no signup. Use it to write letters, reports, essays, blog drafts, study notes, or anything else that needs formatting.

Unlike a plain text notepad, WordPad supports rich text — bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, multiple fonts, custom text and highlight colors, headings, bulleted and numbered lists, indentation, and alignment. You can also insert links, images, and horizontal rules for structure.

Everything happens locally in your browser. Your documents are auto-saved to local storage on your own device. No accounts, no servers, no tracking — and once the page loads, WordPad even works offline.

When you're ready to share or archive your work, export it as plain text (.txt), formatted HTML, or use the Print button to save it as a PDF from your browser's print dialog.
