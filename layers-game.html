What is it?

The Free Layers Game

Layers is a free word puzzle that stacks four mini-games on top of each other and asks you to connect them at the end. It's a daily-style puzzle with no daily limit — a layered challenge you can play as often as you like.

Each puzzle is made of five layers: a word ladder, a letter swap, a hidden word, an anagram, and a final Connections-style sorting board. Every mini-game gives you a clue word. When you've collected them all, you group those words (plus a few extras) into four hidden categories of four.

It's the best of both worlds: the puzzle variety of a mini-game collection with the aha-moment satisfaction of a Connections puzzle. Everything runs in your browser — no signup, no ads in the game, and your streak is stored locally on your device.

Whether you're a Wordle veteran, a Connections fanatic, or just looking for a new daily brain-teaser, Layers gives you a fresh challenge every time you press New Puzzle.
