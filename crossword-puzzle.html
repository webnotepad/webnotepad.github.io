What is it?

The Free Online Crossword Puzzle

A crossword puzzle is a word game where you fill a grid of white and black squares with answers to numbered clues. Clues are split into two lists: Across for horizontal words and Down for vertical words. Where words cross, the same letter must fit both answers — that interlock is what makes crosswords both tricky and deeply satisfying to solve.

Our free crossword game gives you six hand-built puzzles from a quick 5×5 Mini to a full Expert grid. Every puzzle uses a clean, readable layout, a live timer, and a two-level hint system. Fill in a single letter with Hint Letter, reveal an entire answer with Reveal Word, or press Check to flag mistakes without spoiling the solution.

The game works equally well with a physical keyboard and with your thumbs on a phone. Arrow keys move the cursor between cells, Enter and Space toggle between Across and Down, and Backspace deletes a letter. On mobile, tapping a square brings up the on-screen keyboard automatically, so you can solve entirely with touch.

Everything happens locally in your browser. Your answers, progress and best times are stored in localStorage only — nothing is sent to our servers. Solve the same puzzle as many times as you like and try to beat your best time.
