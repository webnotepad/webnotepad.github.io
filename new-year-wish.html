What is it?

The Free New Year Wish Maker

New Year Wish Maker is a free browser-based tool for creating personalized New Year cards. Add the recipient's name, your own name and a custom message, pick one of six themes, and the live preview updates instantly.

The magic is in the shareable link. As you edit, the details are encoded into the URL hash. When you copy the link, anyone who opens it sees the exact same personalized card — no login, no database, no server. It is a self-contained wish that travels with the link.

Need inspiration? Use the one-click presets for friends, family, colleagues and romantic partners to fill in a warm message, then tweak it to sound like you. When the card is ready, copy the link, download it as an image, or both.

Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Your wish stays private until you choose to share it.
