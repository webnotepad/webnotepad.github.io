Understanding Artifacts

How Artifact Enhancement Works.

Every artifact levels with Artifact EXP, and every point of EXP also costs Mora. A 5★ artifact needs 270,475 EXP to reach +20, so a full five-piece set takes more than 1.35 million EXP and the same amount of Mora.

You can feed an artifact with Sanctifying Unction (2,500 EXP) or Sanctifying Essence (10,000 EXP), or with other artifacts as fodder. Fodder gives its base EXP plus 80% of what was already invested in it, and you only pay Mora for the base value.

That makes a +4 or +8 artifact far cheaper per EXP than a fresh one, which is why this calculator compares every material side by side.

Upgrade Guide

Spend EXP Efficiently.

Artifact EXP is usually scarcer than Mora, so the goal is to waste as little as possible.

Tips

  • Upgrade to +4 first to reveal the extra substat before investing further, and only continue with pieces worth keeping.
  • Feed leveled artifacts as fodder. A +4 5★ gives 16,820 EXP for just 3,780 Mora.
  • Use Sanctifying Essence and Unction on pieces you are sure about, since they cost 1 Mora per EXP.
  • Convert unwanted artifacts with salvage into Unction and Essence instead of discarding them.
  • Domains are an efficient EXP source because every run drops artifacts that also feed your upgrades.
