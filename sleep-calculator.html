Why it matters

The Science of Sleep Cycles

Sleep isn't just one long, uniform state — it's a series of 90-minute cycles that move through light sleep, deep sleep, and REM (dreaming) sleep. Waking up in the middle of a cycle often leaves you groggy and disoriented, even after a full night's rest.

Our sleep calculator uses the timing of these cycles to recommend bedtimes and wake-up times that align with the natural end of a cycle. When you wake at the end of a cycle, you're more likely to feel refreshed and alert.

Each person's sleep needs vary by age. Adults typically need 7–9 hours, teenagers 8–10 hours, and younger children even more. The calculator factors in your age group to suggest the right number of cycles.

It also accounts for how long it takes you to fall asleep — usually 10 to 20 minutes. That way, your recommended bedtime is when you should get into bed, not when you should be asleep.
