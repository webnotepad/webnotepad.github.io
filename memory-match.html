How it works

Play Memory Match in 3 simple steps.

01

Flip Two Cards

Click or tap any card to flip it over and reveal the SVG symbol on the front. Flip a second card to try and find its matching pair.

02

Find Matching Pairs

If both cards show the same symbol, they stay face-up and count as a matched pair. If they don't match, they flip back after a short delay.

03

Match Them All

Find all matching pairs to win. The game tracks your time and moves. Try to beat your personal best for each difficulty level!

What is it?

The Free Online Memory Match Game

Memory Match (also known as Concentration or simply the memory game) is a classic card game that challenges your short-term memory and visual recognition. The rules are simple: flip two cards at a time to find matching pairs. Every match stays revealed, and the goal is to clear the entire board.

Our free online memory game features beautiful hand-crafted SVG card designs — no generic emoji, no boring clip-art. Each symbol is a custom vector illustration that looks crisp on any screen, from a small phone to a large desktop monitor.

Play across three difficulty levels: Easy (6 pairs, 4×3 grid), Medium (8 pairs, 4×4 grid), and Hard (12 pairs, 6×4 grid). Your best time for each level is saved in your browser, so you can compete against yourself and watch your memory improve over time.

Memory games are more than just fun — they're a proven way to improve concentration, sharpen short-term memory, and keep your brain active. Whether you're a student taking a study break, an adult looking for a quick mental workout, or a senior keeping your mind sharp, this card matching game is a perfect choice. It's fully mobile-friendly, so you can play on the bus, in a waiting room, or on the couch.
